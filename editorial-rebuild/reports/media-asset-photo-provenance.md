They are not photographs of KOTTO's actual office, employees, customers, equipment, finished customer work, or eyewear products offered for sale.

The original images remain in `/workspace/generated_images/`. Delivered WebP files retain the complete generated composition; only format compression was applied. Both compressed files were opened with `view_image` for visual inspection.

## Content and safety review

Neither image includes customer names, Korean text, readable performance claims, personal information, third-party logos, operating history, prices, or customer testimonials. No existing customer imagery was provided to or edited by the generation tool. Neither asset copies an external campaign. The small photographic prints inside the pictures are generated too.

Keep actual customer cases visually and editorially distinct from these conceptual images. When the surrounding heading already communicates the meaning and the picture is purely scenic, an empty `alt` is appropriate. Do not describe generation artifacts or invented material geometry as a factual technical illustration.
