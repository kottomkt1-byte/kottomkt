# ZIN 01 — photo-based 3D recreation

This model was built for Kotto's eyewear detail presentation from Gentle
Monster's public ZIN 01 product photography and published dimensions.
It is a reconstruction, not manufacturer CAD, a scan, or a virtual-try-on asset.
The reference photographs are not embedded in the GLB or distributed here.

Reference product: Gentle Monster ZIN 01 (진 01), black acetate front,
silver titanium temples, clear lenses.

Official product page:
https://www.gentlemonster.com/int/ko/item/ZYYE799SURVC/zin01

Published dimensions in millimeters:
- Front width: 147.2
- Lens width: 52.7
- Lens height: 40.9
- Bridge: 21
- Temple length: 149.1

The front silhouette and lens openings are traced from the official frontal
photograph. Front depth, wrap, bevels, lens curvature, hinges, adjustable pads,
and the silver temple details are reconstructed using the angle and side
photographs; those reconstructed measurements are approximate.

The studio poster and interactive view use the same locally authored geometry.
Geometry units are meters; glTF +Y is up and +Z faces forward.

The older `eyewear-studio.glb` and its license files remain as an archived asset.
Its Khronos attribution does not describe this ZIN 01 recreation.
